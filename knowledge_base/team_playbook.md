## Introduction

This Team Playbook is for all team members at Purpose Unlimited and its affiliates (collectively known throughout the playbook as **Purpose**). We are thrilled to have you as part of our team. Your dedication and hard work are instrumental to our success, and we deeply appreciate the contributions you bring. For new team members, this playbook is designed to help you understand our company, our values, and to support your integration into the team.

At Purpose, we believe that when an industry stops innovating, it starts stagnating. That's why we founded Purpose: to create a seismic shift that puts people at the center of financial services. Our vision is simple yet bold: solve real human problems and help people succeed. Every solution we build is driven by innovation, determination, and a commitment to creating new pathways to success for those willing to take calculated risks and embrace change.

We are on a mission to reinvent financial services for those excited about tomorrow. With the team, vision, and motivation we have today, we are confident in our ability to make the shift happen.

The principles that underlie this playbook are based on accountability, respect for all, and our confidence in our team members as individuals and as part of an extended family. The following guidelines are not intended to cover all the policies and procedures that must be observed here at Purpose. We therefore reserve the right to add to and/or modify the content of the playbook.

If you have any questions or need additional information, please reach out to the People & Culture Team. They are here to help.

We look forward to your success and to building a future together at Purpose Unlimited.

## Code of Conduct

> *We are judged on the collective and individual actions of our team members.*

**All team members must:**

- Disclose all potential conflicts of interest.
- Avoid situations that might lead to a conflict of interest or suspicion of a conflict of interest.
- Comply with applicable rules and regulations of federal, provincial and local governments, and other regulatory agencies.
- Maintain a safe and healthy work environment pursuant to the Anti-Harassment, Retaliation, Discrimination, and Violence Policy and all applicable provincial and federal legislation.
- Maintain a scent-free workplace.
- Not accept gifts, trips, entertainment, or favours from a customer, potential customer, supplier, or potential supplier of goods that do not align with your company's specific compliance policies with respect to gifts and entertainment.
- Not make recordings or transcriptions of meetings or conversations without prior notification.
- Ensure conduct outside of work does not bring the reputation of Purpose into disrepute.
- Report any regulatory sanctions or conviction of a crime immediately to the Chief People Officer.
- Respect the confidentiality of information acquired during employment.
- Provide clients with information that is accurate, complete, objective, relevant, timely, and understandable.
- Not discriminate in interactions with team members and external contacts, based on race, colour, ancestry, place of origin, political belief, religion, marital status, family status, physical or mental disability, sex, sexual orientation or age or other grounds protected by the Human Rights Code.
- Be mindful of dressing in an appropriate way when working from the office or in a client/partner-facing capacity.
- Adhere to a standard of professional conduct and integrity. This ensures that the work environment is safe, comfortable and productive. Team members should be respectful, courteous, and mindful of others' feelings and needs.

Please note that if you are employed by a regulated entity, there may be additional responsibilities you will need to adhere to. Please refer to your legal entity's Compliance Manual if applicable.

## Diversity (DEIB)

Purpose is committed to creating and sustaining an inclusive and equitable environment where all individuals feel welcomed, valued, and empowered to contribute their best. Our people are our most valuable resource. The collective sum of the individual differences, life experiences, knowledge, creativity, self-expression, unique capabilities and talent that our team members invest in their work represents a significant part of what makes Purpose unique.

We embrace the differences that make up our people and welcome how those differences have shaped their perspectives. Whether that is a difference in age, color, disability, ethnicity, family or marital status, gender identity or expression, language, national origin, physical and mental ability, political affiliation, race, religion, sexual orientation, socio-economic status, and other characteristics — we support what makes our team members unique.

To sustain a culture of high performance and growth, we must ensure that our people are assessed fairly, consistently, and without bias. A true meritocracy leaves no room for distorted perceptions. Additionally, we believe that cognitive diversity is a strategic advantage — fueling innovation, unlocking creativity, and driving stronger results. We believe in a growth culture that thrives on unique perspectives and empowers every team member to contribute, learn, develop, innovate and achieve their full potential.

### Definitions

- **Diversity:** The presence of differences within our organization, recognizing and valuing a broad spectrum of backgrounds and identities.
- **Equity:** The commitment to fair treatment, access, and opportunity, while actively working to remove barriers to advancement for marginalized and underrepresented groups.
- **Inclusion:** Creating intentional practices so every individual feels respected, heard, and able to contribute fully to organizational success.
- **Belonging:** The intersection where individual views, beliefs and values are integrated, resulting in a culture of innovation.

### Review and Assessment

We take a holistic view of Purpose's current and future state through: data analysis, independent expert evaluations, eNPS insights, anecdotal feedback, and a review of our organizational processes and practices. This comprehensive assessment ensures our DEIB strategy is grounded in evidence and aligned with our ambition to lead with courage and inclusivity. These reviews are ongoing and monitored on an ongoing basis.

### Policy Guidelines

**Equal Opportunity & Fair Practices**

- We provide equal opportunities in recruitment, hiring, advancement, compensation, and all employment practices.
- Decisions are based solely on qualifications, performance, and organizational needs, without regard to any protected characteristic.

**Inclusive Recruitment & Hiring**

- Recruiting strategies include sourcing from multiple sites to expand our talent pool, targeting specific channels to attract a more diverse profile of applicants to the top of our funnel.
- Hiring teams have incorporated structured, scorecard-based interview practices to reduce implicit bias.
- Gender-neutral language is used in job postings.

**Workplace Culture & Professional Development**

- Career development programs to provide equitable paths for growth and advancement.

**Compensation & Benefits**

- Regular pay audits to identify and close wage gaps.
- Flexible work policies designed to support team members' life circumstances.

**Accountability & Measurement**

- DEIB goals are clearly communicated, measurable, and reviewed annually.
- Progress is tracked and reported to all stakeholders, with accountability for leaders and managers to foster a DEIB-centered culture.

### Reporting & Resolution

Accessible, confidential channels exist for reporting discrimination, harassment, or policy violations. All concerns are investigated promptly, with corrective action when necessary.

## Team Member Resource Groups

Team Member Resource Groups (TMRGs) are integral to Purpose's commitment to helping us drive a community rooted in diversity, equity, and inclusion.

TMRGs are team member-led, self-directed voluntary groups that focus on shared experiences or common interests. They offer opportunities to network internally, provide an open forum of shared understanding, and create mentoring and career development opportunities. They can also foster positive and inclusive environments for all team members and influence policies and procedures that make a more equitable and welcoming community to work in.

### Examples of TMRGs

- Women at Purpose
- BIPOC at Purpose
- BIPOC Tech at Purpose
- Pride at Purpose
- Parents at Purpose

Team Member Resource Groups are not those who share common hobbies or interests such as Dungeons and Dragons, golf, or hockey. While creating these types of groups/Slack channels are welcome and encouraged, they are not the focus or the intention behind TMRGs.

### Team members who join TMRGs can

- Serve as drivers for diversity, equity, and inclusion at Purpose and help identify opportunities for us to become more inclusive.
- Identify best practices throughout the organization.
- Host or attend social or networking events related to their TMRG.

TMRGs are open to all Purpose team members voluntarily.

### How to start a TMRG

Groups seeking information to form a TMRG can reach out to the People & Culture Team at **PeopleCultureTeam@purpose-unlimited.com**. Purpose has budgeted amounts for diversity, equity, inclusion, and belonging events and initiatives.

## Interpersonal Relationships

Purpose strongly believes that a work environment where team members maintain clear boundaries between personal and business interactions is necessary for effective business operations. Although this policy does not prevent the development of friendships or romantic relationships between co-workers, it does establish boundaries as to how relationships are conducted during working hours and within the working environment.

- Managers and direct reports may not engage in an interpersonal or sexual relationship.
- Team members who become involved in a romantic relationship must disclose it to a member of the People & Culture Team.
- Both parties must confirm that they are in a consensual relationship by signing off on a consent form.
- During working time and in working areas, team members are expected to conduct themselves in an appropriate workplace manner that does not interfere with others or with overall productivity and does not put others in an uncomfortable position.

Team members are strictly prohibited from engaging in physical contact that would in any way be deemed inappropriate in the workplace by a reasonable person while anywhere on company premises, whether during working hours or not.

Team member off-duty conduct is generally regarded as private, as long as such conduct does not create issues within the workplace. The provisions of this policy apply regardless of the sexual orientation or gender identification of the parties involved.

Individuals in regulated positions are required to disclose any interpersonal relationship that:

- Involves oversight, approval, audit, compliance review, investigation, or risk assessment.
- Could reasonably be perceived to impair independence, objectivity, or regulatory compliance.

Disclosures must be made to the People & Culture Team and the appropriate Chief Compliance Officer upon formation of the relationship or upon a change in role or reporting structure.

## Hiring Family Members Policy

We are honoured that our team members recommend Purpose to family members, and we want to ensure that everyone has a great experience with us. In line with this, we would like to share with you our guidelines for hiring team members' relatives in our company.

'Relative' in this context is defined as a person who is connected by blood or marriage. It includes a broader range of relationships and can encompass family members as well as extended family, such as aunts, uncles, cousins, grandparents, siblings or parents-in-law, amongst other examples.

For a Relative to be hired at Purpose, the candidate, team members, and roles must comply with the following points:

- **Proactive Disclosure:** Candidates must always disclose if they have Relatives who work at Purpose and the type of relationship. Similarly, team members must always disclose to People & Culture if they are aware that a relative is part of our recruitment process, and the type of relationship.
- **Conflict of Interest:** No team member shall be involved in the hiring, promotion, or evaluation of a Relative.
- **Reporting Relationship:** Candidates cannot be hired when the role they applied for rolls up to a relative, even if indirectly.
- **Legacy Cases:** Team members hired by Purpose before the communication of our Family Member Guidelines that are not in compliance with the current terms will be grandfathered under our previous practices, continuing in their current role and reporting structure.

## Alcohol, Cannabis & Drug-Free Environment

We are committed to maintaining a safe, productive, and respectful workplace for all team members. To support this, we uphold strict guidelines regarding the use of alcohol, cannabis, and controlled substances.

- No consuming, distributing, possessing, selling, or being under the influence of any form of controlled substance(s) in the workplace, or while engaged in work functions, will be permitted.
- During corporate events or social activities where alcohol is served, team members are expected to use proper judgment.
- Team members have a duty to report a team member if they are intoxicated or under the influence of drugs or alcohol in the workplace.
- Anyone who is suffering from a drug or alcohol addiction or who has reasonable grounds to believe that another team member may be addicted has the duty to inform a member of the Legal group or the People & Culture Team immediately. We offer coverage for mental health practitioners through our paramedical benefits, which provide access to confidential support and assistance in such situations.
- Consuming any form of cannabis for recreational purposes before the start of the work day, or while engaged in organized events or activities, is prohibited.
- Some prescription drugs cause impairment. Contact a member of the People & Culture Team and provide documents justifying the reason for use. They will provide accommodation as required.

## Expenses – Travel

### Eligibility

If full-time team members are required to travel for business purposes, the following general guidelines outline the expenses that Purpose will cover. Please connect with the Office Management team before booking travel to learn more about our corporate rates and booking services. All work-related travel is required to be booked exclusively using the **Melon app**, the company's approved travel platform. Using Melon ensures centralized oversight, streamlined expense tracking, and compliance with travel policies. Booking through alternative platforms is strongly discouraged and may result in delays or issues with reimbursement and reporting.

Contractors must obtain prior approval from their designated manager before booking or planning any work-related travel. This approval must include a description of the business purpose for the travel and a review of the estimated costs. This ensures alignment with project needs, budget considerations, and organizational priorities. Travel arrangements made without documented approval may not be reimbursed. For contractors paid through payroll, it is preferred that they submit their own expenses directly through Zoho. For contractors who invoice, reimbursable expenses should be included with their invoice, along with appropriate proof of payment.

If a traveller identifies significant cost savings by booking outside of Melon, such as through personal rewards programs or promotional offers, they must provide clear documentation of the savings opportunity when submitting for reimbursement. This documentation should include a comparison of Melon pricing versus the alternative and must be submitted alongside the travel expense claim for review and approval.

**Please note:** While the Melon App is the preferred platform for booking travel, an exception is currently in place for train tickets. As the Melon App does not yet support train ticket purchases, team members may use alternative methods to book train travel. Be sure to retain and submit all relevant receipts and documentation with your expense report.

Melon should be used for Business Travel only. Personal travel bookings are not permitted through this platform. For any questions related to Melon, please reach out to **office@purpose.ca**.

### Reimbursement

All expenses require an expense report, which must be completed through the online Zoho portal. Expense reports must be submitted within **60 days** of incurring the expense and must include a copy of the original detailed receipt. Please note that expense reports may be denied if these guidelines are not followed.

Whenever possible, travel should be booked in advance to help secure lower rates and ensure cost-effective planning. If you have any questions or are unsure about a specific expense, we encourage you to reach out to your manager or the Finance team for clarification before submitting.

### Air Travel

- Economy, considering the convenience of direct flights or limited connections.
- Cancellation or re-booking fee should be waived if a low-cost ticket needs to be changed due to a valid business reason.
- Baggage fees if the airline imposes a fee for the first bag, and will reimburse for the second bag if a team member is travelling for more than five days.
- In-flight WIFI.

**Costs not covered:**

- In-flight entertainment.
- Seat selection.
- Fee-based priority or fast lanes for check-in processes or going through airport security.
- Airline club membership fees/dues or lounge privileges.
- Luggage delivery services to/from the airport to the hotel/home.
- Additional pricing to accommodate or accumulate frequent flyer points.

**Business Class is permitted under the following circumstances:**

- An upgrade by the airline at no additional cost.
- Team member pays for the upgrade using cash or frequent flyer points.
- Business requirement to accompany a client, provided pre-approval is obtained by your manager.

### Train Travel

- Team members may travel in Business Class within Canada.

### Cars

- Reasonable parking charges while driving for business.
- Toll charges for business use (i.e., the only route available).
- Use of a personal vehicle for business purposes will be reimbursed for mileage at the current automobile allowance rate as stipulated by the CRA.

**Please note:** The company is not responsible for any speeding tickets, parking infractions, or other traffic violations incurred during business travel. These are considered personal liabilities and will not be reimbursed.

### Taxis / Uber / Lyft

- Taxis (or ride share) may be taken for business purposes within the city limits, with a receipt.
- Team members returning home after leaving a company-sponsored event where alcohol was served.

### UP Express

- Team members coming from Pearson International Airport to work are expected to use the UP Express train where possible.

### Hotel / Lodging

- Business accommodations may also be booked by team members themselves. All considerations, including the team members' comfort, location, cost and length of stay, will be partial to the decision of accommodations.
- To determine appropriate daily hotel accommodation costs, please refer to the guidelines provided by Melon. Any bookings that exceed these guidelines require prior approval.
- We encourage the use of hotels in Montreal, Toronto, and Vancouver, where corporate rates have been negotiated (contact office managers for details).

### Meals When Traveling

- When team members are travelling out of town for business purposes.
- Up to **$75 per day**, excluding taxes and tip.
- Tipping expenses should be included in your expense report and must be accompanied by a receipt or a note indicating the amount and purpose of the tip.
- Please note: the $75 daily limit applies to the base cost of meals. Taxes and tips may be claimed in addition, provided they are reasonable, properly documented, and tips do not exceed **18%** of the meal cost.

### Laundry & Dry Cleaning

- Permitted when a business trip is planned for more than 7 days – **max $20 per week**.

### Cellular Phones

- Up to **$100 before tax** for phone usage costs incurred while travelling.

### Vacation With Travel

- The business portion of the travel will be covered.
- No incremental costs associated with personal vacation will be covered.

### Conference Fees

- All conference registrations must be approved in advance by your manager and/or the Finance team to ensure alignment with business goals and budget availability.
- Purpose will cover reasonable registration fees for conferences that are directly related to your role, professional development, or industry relevance.
- A copy of the conference agenda and registration confirmation must be submitted along with your expense report in the Zoho portal.
- Team members are encouraged to register early to take advantage of discounted rates and secure availability.
- Optional add-ons such as gala dinners, leisure activities, or upgrades not essential to the conference may not be reimbursed unless explicitly approved.
- Registration fees for virtual conferences are also eligible for reimbursement, subject to the same approval and documentation requirements.
- If travel is required for the conference, all related expenses (hotel, transportation, meals) must follow the general travel policy guidelines and be booked through the Melon app.

## Flexible Paid Time Off

Purpose believes that vacation time is important, providing you with time away from work to relax and enjoy yourself, pursue personal interests, and recharge. That's why we offer a flexible paid time off approach!

Trust is paramount in any good professional relationship. We believe in our team members' ability to use their judgment when it comes to managing their time and taking the time they need away from work. It is a team member's duty to ensure that objectives and deadlines are met. We believe that a successful flexible paid time off policy contributes to a culture of responsibility and mutual respect, which can boost both productivity and morale.

### Taking Time Off

Vacation scheduling is critical to proper planning at Purpose. You are responsible for ensuring (i) that you take vacation without impacting your ability to execute effectively and to the level expected; (ii) that you provide sufficient advanced notice to your manager/team; (iii) that consecutive weeks off do not exceed **2 weeks**, to ensure business continuity and avoid a significant burden to your team.

Vacation time must be entered into Dayforce and approved by your Manager prior to booking it. If your vacation plans change, please update Dayforce accordingly.

It is also important that you take at least the minimum amount of vacation time required under the Provincial employment standards within the province in which you reside.

### Manager's Responsibility

It is a manager's responsibility to ensure that team members take at least the minimum amount of vacation time required under the Acts.

While this policy provides flexibility, team members may take a maximum of **2 consecutive weeks** off at a time. Managers may reject leave requests at their discretion if:

- Other team members with similar or complementary duties have already requested leave for that period, and approvals are granted on a first-come, first-served basis.
- The time in question is too busy or includes an important deadline for the team member asking for leave.
- A team member appears to abuse the policy by not respecting the processes in place.

The People & Culture Business Partners will monitor Dayforce for minimum vacation usage.

### Eligibility

All permanent, full-time team members are eligible. Students, part-time and contractual team members are subject to the number of days/weeks in their employment agreement.

### End of Employment Relationship

At the end of the employment relationship, any unused accrued vacation (calculated based on the minimum statutory amount according to employment standards) will be paid out with your final pay. Your final vacation accrual balance will include any vacation earned during a statutory notice or pay in lieu of notice period.

## Sick Days, Disability & Extended Leave

### Sick Days

We believe that it is best for everyone if you stay home to recover when you are unwell. You may need to take sick days periodically. Sick days are included in this flexible paid time off policy. Should you need to take time off because you are sick, you must notify your manager as soon as possible and enter your time in Dayforce.

Under certain circumstances, team members may be asked to submit a physician's note or other medical certification for their absence.

Sick time requests will be handled with discretion. Failure to notify your manager is deemed as abandonment of duties and may result in disciplinary action.

Extended sick leaves should be reported to your People & Culture Business Partner and will be handled on a case-by-case basis. In cases of very lengthy illnesses, an application for short term sick leave may be requested.

### Short Term Disability

Team members may apply for Short-Term Disability through Canada Life. All notices must include a medical certificate.

The short-term disability plan provides **67% of weekly earnings after a 6-day waiting period, with a maximum benefit of $1,000 per week for up to 17 weeks**.

### Long Term Disability

Long-Term disability occurs in the event of a prolonged illness or injury which exceeds seventeen (17) consecutive weeks.

All team members enrolled under the group insurance plan have long-term disability (LTD) insurance coverage. For more information regarding such coverage, please review your League/Canada Life Coverage.

### Extended Leave

Instances of extended leaves of absence may occur for various reasons. The following policy is intended to assist those who are eligible for certain types of leave, including: Maternity, Paternity and Parental leave, Sick leave, and Family Medical & Compassionate Care leave.

Purpose operates in accordance with applicable provincial labour laws and employment standards when a team member is eligible to legislated types of extended absences.

When an absence leads to an interruption of pay, Purpose will issue a Record of Employment (ROE) for the team member to be able to apply for relevant coverage.

**Departure and Return:** Notify your manager as soon as you know the date of departure and return. You will be returned to the same position that you were in or a comparable position. Throughout the extended leave, team members' access to Slack and email will be cut, and information will be redirected to their manager and/or team.

**Benefits:** Arrangements regarding team member paid benefits must be made between Payroll and the team member prior to departure.

## Pregnancy, Parental and Paternity Leave

This policy applies to all Purpose team members who are "parents".

A "parent" includes:

- a birth parent;
- an adoptive parent; or
- a person who is in a relationship of some permanence with a parent of the child and who plans on treating the child as their own.

New parents have the right to take parental leave – unpaid time off work when a baby or child is born or first comes into their care. Purpose offers a top-up program for expectant mothers.

### Maternity Leave

Maternity leave is available only to the person who is away from work because they are pregnant or have recently given birth, and it cannot be shared between parents. The individual receiving maternity benefits may also be entitled to parental benefits. Purpose provides a maternity leave top-up for several weeks (see the [Benefits – Health & Wellness Program](#benefits--health--wellness-program) section, *Parental Leave Salary Top-Up*, for details).

When you are ready, please notify your Manager of your anticipated leave date. Purpose requires **8–12 weeks' notice** to plan appropriate coverage. After informing your leader, contact your People & Culture Business Partner with your prospective maternity or parental leave start date, including any time off you may be taking beforehand. Please note that while you are on leave, your equipment and system access will be removed for the duration of your leave. When you are ready to return, notify us at least **four weeks** in advance.

### Parental Leave

- **Standard parental benefits** (up to 40 weeks, but one parent cannot receive more than 35 weeks of standard benefits). From Service Canada, you will receive up to a maximum of **55% of your salary** at the beginning of each year.
- **Extended parental benefits** (up to 69 weeks, but one parent cannot receive more than 61 weeks of extended benefits). From Service Canada, you will receive up to a maximum of **33% of your salary** at the beginning of each year.

Your choice determines the number of weeks and the weekly amount you'll receive. If sharing, each parent must choose the same option and submit their own application. Parents can receive their weeks of benefits at the same time or one after another.

Once you start receiving parental benefits, you cannot change options.

**References:**

- Government of Canada Parental Leave: https://www.canada.ca/en/services/benefits/ei/ei-maternity-parental.html
- Government of Quebec Parental Insurance Plan: https://www.rqap.gouv.qc.ca/en/online-services/applying-for-benefits

## Other Leaves of Absence

### Family Medical & Compassionate Care Leave

Team members may be absent from work to fulfill obligations related to the care, health or education of their child or the child of their spouse or due to the state of health of their spouse, father or mother, brother or sister, or grandparent, in accordance with provincial legislation.

### Bereavement Leave

Purpose understands that sometimes, major life circumstances such as a death in the family may come before work. The passing of a loved one is never easy nor expected, and we understand these difficult moments. As such, we provide team members the opportunity to take time off in these difficult periods. Bereavement leave falls under the flexible paid time off policy.

### Jury / Witness Duty Leave

Regular full-time team members are eligible for Jury Duty Leave. Team members called for Jury Duty will be eligible for **100% base pay**. 1. When an incident occurs, the BCP Head, Beverley McKenzie, or a designated team lead (based on who is in the office) will send a notification to the PU Emergency Slack channel. 3. The Head of BCP and/or the Office Administrators will communicate with all People Managers via Slack, email, or phone to ensure they are aware of the incident and understand the required next steps.

6. The Head of BCP will notify all employees through the appropriate communication channels when the incident has been resolved. They will confirm team member status and locations, oversee the resumption of normal work, and conduct an incident review and prepare a report.

## Respect in the Workplace

### Anti-Harassment, Retaliation, Discrimination, and Violence Policy

Purpose is committed to maintaining a safe, respectful, and inclusive workplace for all team members. Team members have a right to a harassment-free workplace, and there is zero tolerance for any form of harassment, discrimination, violence, or retaliation. This includes, but is not limited to, bullying, sexual harassment, and discriminatory behaviour based on race, colour, religion, gender, gender identity or expression, nationality, age, disability, marital status, sexual orientation, or any other ground protected by applicable human rights law.

This policy applies to all team members including permanent full-time and part-time members, contractors, interns, and third-party agency staff who have entered into an independent contractor agreement with Purpose. This policy also covers all work-related settings, including remote and virtual environments (inclusive of Slack, Teams, email, and other electronic forms of communication), client and work events. It applies to interactions between team members, leaders, clients, suppliers, visitors, and any external individuals with a relationship to Purpose.

### Definitions of Workplace Misconduct

**Harassment** is any unwelcome comment, conduct, or behaviour that demeans, humiliates, intimidates, or threatens a team member, affecting their dignity, safety, or psychological or physical integrity. It may be verbal, written, physical, virtual or visual, and can occur in person, electronically, or through social media. It may involve repeated behaviour or, in some cases, a single serious incident that has a lasting harmful effect. Harassment includes psychological harassment, bullying, and workplace sexual harassment.

- **Psychological Harassment:** Repeated, hostile, or unwanted behaviour that could reasonably be expected to harm a team member's dignity or create a negative work environment. A single serious incident may also qualify if it has lasting harmful effects. Examples include verbal abuse or threats.
- **Bullying:** Includes any inappropriate conduct or comment that causes or could reasonably be expected to cause humiliation or intimidation, such as spreading rumours, or participating in sabotage. Bullying does not include reasonable management actions taken in good faith to address work performance, or workplace conduct.
- **Workplace Sexual Harassment:** Includes any of the following: (i) unwelcome sexual advances, requests, remarks, sexualized jokes or innuendo; (ii) comments or conduct related to sex, sexual orientation, gender identity, or gender expression that are known or ought reasonably to be known to be unwelcome; or (iii) any sexual solicitation or advance made by someone in a position to confer, grant, or deny a workplace benefit, where it is known or ought reasonably to be known to be unwelcome.

**Retaliation** is any adverse action taken against a team member for making a complaint, participating in an investigation, or exercising rights under this Policy or applicable legislation. Retaliation is strictly prohibited.

**Discrimination** is any adverse treatment, exclusion, or impact on a team member that is based on a protected ground under applicable human rights legislation, and that results in a denial of equal opportunity or benefit in employment. This does not prohibit reasonable accommodations designed to comply with human rights obligations.

**Violence** is any act of physical force, attempted physical force, or threat of physical force against a team member in the workplace that causes or could cause physical injury. For clarity, this includes verbal threats, aggressive behaviour, or any conduct that a team member could reasonably interpret as creating a risk of physical harm. Workplace violence also includes domestic or intimate partner violence when it occurs in the workplace.

### Prevention and Responsibilities

Purpose will take reasonable precautions to prevent harassment, discrimination, and violence. All new team members will complete mandatory training as part of their onboarding. Current team members are required to participate in mandatory training and refresher sessions in accordance with provincial regulatory requirements. Team members are expected to refrain from Workplace Misconduct and to report any concerns or incidents to their leader or the People & Culture Team. All team members are encouraged to speak up without fear of retaliation.

### Reporting and Complaint Process

Any team member who experiences or witnesses misconduct should report it promptly, either verbally or in writing, to their leader or People & Culture. If a team member is uncomfortable reporting to their leader, they may contact People & Culture directly. Complaints may be raised informally or formally, and all concerns will be investigated fairly, promptly, and as confidentially as possible. It is important to note that if there is suspicion of a policy violation (such as a concern about behaviour being shared) a formal complaint is not required for Purpose to act. Purpose has an obligation to investigate any potential violations of this policy.

Team members are protected from reprisal for making good faith complaints, participating in investigations, supporting others, or refusing to tolerate harassment. Retaliation, such as dismissal, demotion, unfair evaluations, exclusion, or intimidation, is not permitted.

### Company Responsibilities

Purpose is committed to preventing harassment, discrimination, and violence by taking proactive measures, responding to concerns promptly, and conducting fair and confidential investigations. When misconduct is substantiated, appropriate corrective or disciplinary action will be taken. The organization will also ensure legal compliance, provide ongoing training, maintain secure and accurate records, and offer support to team members throughout the complaint process.

### Leader Responsibilities

Leaders are expected to model respectful behaviour at Purpose. If they witness, become aware of, or receive a concern about conduct that may violate this policy, they are responsible for addressing the situation promptly and reporting it through the appropriate channels. Leaders must also support any resulting investigation and ensure that corrective actions are implemented when required.

In addition, leaders are expected to promote awareness of this policy, encourage open communication, and help team members understand their rights and responsibilities.

Failure to fulfill these responsibilities may result in disciplinary action.

### Team Member Responsibilities

Team members are expected to refrain from any form of misconduct, report concerns promptly and fully cooperate with investigations. They should maintain professionalism, speak up or signal disapproval of inappropriate behaviour when it is safe to do so, and seek support or guidance whenever needed.

### Performance Management vs. Harassment

Leaders are expected to provide clear, objective, and respectful feedback focused on behaviours and performance outcomes, not personal traits. Disciplinary actions and performance management must serve legitimate workplace purposes and be conducted professionally. Abusive, demeaning, or hostile conduct is not tolerated and may constitute harassment.

### Investigation and Confidentiality

All complaints, informal or formal, will be investigated thoroughly and confidentially, except where disclosure is required by law or necessary to protect safety. The names of those involved will be kept confidential to the extent feasible, in accordance with applicable laws. Purpose will take appropriate action based on the outcome of the investigation.

### Corrective and Disciplinary Action

If misconduct is substantiated, appropriate disciplinary action will be taken, up to and including termination. Disciplinary measures may also be taken against any team member who makes a complaint in bad faith. Management will ensure that any issues are resolved and do not recur.

Disciplinary actions may include coaching or verbal warnings for minor issues, written warnings for repeated or serious behaviour, improvement plans with clear objectives, suspension (with or without pay) for serious misconduct, and termination for severe or repeated violations.

Complaints made maliciously or with the intent to harm may result in disciplinary action. However, complaints lacking evidence or not substantiated are not considered bad faith.

### Legal Compliance

Purpose complies with all relevant laws and regulations, including consulting with the Joint Health and Safety Committee in Ontario and maintaining procedures, training, and reporting mechanisms in British Columbia, and adhering to psychological harassment prevention requirements under Quebec's Act Respecting Labour Standards.

### Support and Accessibility

Support and accommodations are available for team members who need assistance during the complaint process, including language or physical accommodations. Team members may also bring complaints to external authorities or governing bodies if they choose.

## Right to Disconnect

Purpose recognizes that each team member's hours of work will vary depending on their position, their specific duties and responsibilities, their individual arrangements with their manager, and operational/business requirements. This policy is therefore intended to ensure that team members are not required to engage in work, including using electronic communications such as emails, telephone calls or text messages in excess of any applicable statutory threshold of daily or weekly hours of work under applicable employment standards legislation.

Purpose does not expect team members to engage in work (including, but not limited to, engaging in work-related communications, sending or reviewing emails or other electronic messages, or participating in telephone calls or video calls) if doing so would violate applicable employment standards legislation.

### Principles

1. Purpose encourages team members to try to schedule meetings, phone calls, and send communications during regular business hours. However, our commitment to allowing flexible working arrangements may result in some team members sending communications at times that are not convenient to the recipient. This may also occur if the sender is in a different time zone than the recipient. In such situations, the sender should give consideration to the timing of their communication.
2. Unless a business, customer/client, operational or staffing need, or the context of a communication, dictates that an immediate response is required, team members should not feel obliged to respond to communications received outside their regular or approved working hours.
3. All team members are expected to activate an automatic response while on vacation or other leave of absence. The automatic response should advise the sender that the recipient is absent from work, including the start and end date of their absence (if known), and provide alternative contact details if applicable.
4. Given the presumption that team members are regularly reviewing their emails, instant messages, and voicemails during regular business hours, team members who are disconnected, with the approval of their manager, for most or all of a business day should consider activating an automatic response.
5. In accordance with Purpose's commitment to flexible work arrangements, nothing in this policy is intended to prohibit a team member from voluntarily deciding for their own personal convenience to perform work outside of regular business hours provided that (a) the work can be performed as efficiently and productively as during regular business hours; and (b) the team member's manager has previously authorized the team member to work flexible hours and/or remotely.
6. It is important that even team members who have an approved flexible or remote working arrangement still monitor their working hours, take their breaks as required by the applicable employment standards legislation, and arrange to disconnect themselves from work before they exceed the applicable statutory threshold of daily or weekly hours of work. Team members who do not comply with applicable hours of work thresholds or other hours of work requirements pursuant to the applicable employment standards legislation must advise their manager in writing the following business day.
7. Although everyone should be mindful of the importance of disconnecting from work, there may be occasions or situations that require a team member to engage in work beyond their regular working hours. While it is not possible to identify every such occasion or situation, some examples would include:
   a. Where it is not reasonably possible to perform the work during regular hours;
   b. Where the work has to be performed on an urgent basis;
   c. Where the work has to be performed in response to an emergency;
   d. Where the team member needs to replace another team member who is unable to work due to illness, injury, or personal emergency;
   e. Where a delay in performing the work would cause material, operational or production delays;
   f. Where a delay in responding would lead to missed customer/client deadlines or frustrate customer/client objectives; and
   g. In response to unforeseen circumstances.
8. Any team member who has concerns regarding their hours of work, ability to manage their workload during their daily or weekly hours of work, or ability to disconnect from work in accordance with this policy, is expected to raise their concerns with their manager.
9. This policy will be interpreted and applied consistently with Purpose's obligations under the applicable employment standards legislation. Subject to compliance with the applicable employment standards legislation, Purpose shall have full discretionary authority to interpret and administer this Policy. Purpose reserves the right to modify, amend, suspend or terminate this Policy at any time, in its sole discretion, without advance notice.

## Security & IT Policies

### Overview

Purpose gives team members access to one or more forms of electronic media and services, including computers, email, messaging, video conferencing, telephones, internet access, etc. Use of these facilities and services is for business use. All users have the responsibility to use these resources in a professional, ethical, and lawful manner. Users of Purpose assets shall have no expectation of privacy, and Purpose reserves the right to inspect information stored on or in transit through any of its assets without notice. It is important that all team members of Purpose adhere to these policies.

Purpose does not permit unnotified recordings or transcriptions of meetings or conversations. Any electronic recording requires prior written authorization from People & Culture. Unauthorized recording may result in disciplinary action, up to and including termination. In exceptional cases, recordings may be allowed for legitimate business reasons with advance notice and participant consent, in compliance with privacy laws.

### Personal Activities

Users may engage in limited personal activities on Purpose's electronic media and services, provided that such use does not:

- Interfere with anyone's work performance
- Cause Purpose to incur additional costs or risks
- Relate to viewing, downloading, distributing or storing any obscene and/or pornographic or discriminatory material or any materials inciting hatred, intolerance and/or violence
- Breach any laws – including downloading copyrighted material, or distributing copy-protected material
- Use the provided devices for personal OBA (Outside Business Activities) work (e.g. Bitmining, etc.)
- Expose or put Purpose at risk of security breaches or vulnerabilities

### Business Activities

All team members are assigned a Purpose email account. The use of your individually assigned email account is solely for conducting business, and therefore, no other email accounts should be used. Purpose is committed to protecting and respecting the confidentiality of the clients, partners, and associates regarding activities, data and other information. It is strictly prohibited to use non-Purpose email accounts when communicating or conducting business on Purpose's behalf.

All email messages passing through the Purpose infrastructure are considered to be business records of the company. Accordingly, they may be used in administrative, judicial or other proceedings.

The company owns any communication sent via email or that is stored on company equipment. Management and other authorized staff have the right to access any material in your email or on your computer at any time and without notice. Please do not consider your electronic communication, storage or access to be private if it is created or stored at work.

### Login Protection

No information related to authentication or authorization should ever be stored in any form on a mobile device. Unencrypted saved passwords, scripts, programs or macros that can enter credentials are expressly forbidden. Password sharing for individual user accounts is not permitted.

Any tokens or other additional authentication devices must not be allowed to remain attached to or in the same bag, case, box, etc., as any mobile device containing Purpose-related Information. Passwords should not contain any variation of personal information and should incorporate at least 3 of 4 character types (uppercase, lowercase, number and special characters) while maintaining more than 8 characters in length. Do not reuse passwords across multiple services, nor store passwords in text files on your computer.

### Acceptable Device and Internet Usage

**Devices:** All team members are responsible for the proper use of their assigned IT devices. Each team member must make every effort to protect information against loss, unauthorized use, access or modification, and is responsible for reporting any breach or suspected violation that may have occurred. Under no circumstances should devices be used by family, friends or anyone not employed by Purpose.

**Internet:** Personal use of Purpose's internet access should be limited and should not affect network performance. All emails and internet usage are monitored and should not be considered private.

Internet use brings the possibility of breaches to the security of confidential company information, and team members must report any device or internet security incidents to IT. All team members must complete the assigned Cybersecurity training at onboarding and on an ongoing, regular basis as dictated by Purpose.

### Personal Mobile Devices – BYOD

Personal mobile devices will adhere to a BYOD (Bring Your Own Device) Policy, allowing access to Purpose information and applications. All devices must be updated with the appropriate security controls to mitigate risks.

As a baseline minimum, team members must ensure that the device is not jailbroken (or rooted with Android) and that software and operating systems are kept up to date by ensuring automatic app updates are enabled. The device must have a strong password (as set out in Login Protection) used to access your mobile device (and/or biometrics).

Do not store work-related files on the device or personally acquired storage (iCloud, Dropbox, etc). If you sell or transfer your phone, ensure its data is wiped properly. Never leave your mobile device unattended in a public space.

### IT & Remote Work

Purpose embraces team member opportunities to work remotely as appropriate. All team members who work remotely must follow the acceptable use of portable computer devices, and when remote working, ensure WIFI networks are secured. Avoid using any public open WIFI; if necessary, ensure IT has set up a proper VPN before doing so.

International remote work will need to be approved by the manager, the security team made aware, and geofencing enabled while working abroad. Team members are prohibited from remotely working in select countries, where the cybersecurity risk is high. Please note that Purpose devices cannot enter these countries for remote work or other reasons.

Users must take due care and attention to secure portable computer devices when moving between home and business offices. Users will not install any software on Purpose owned portable computer devices. Users will not change the configuration of any Purpose owned portable computer devices. User requests for hardware or software must be approved by the user's manager. Equipment and software will then be purchased and installed by IT. If travelling, remote access must be approved by your leader, People & Culture, and IT to allow access, especially depending on destination. Geofencing is in place to restrict access.

Full details on Security & IT policies will be accessible via Dayforce, and any required training will be communicated separately.

### Protection of Confidential Information

No confidential information is to be held on any portable or mobile device without the express permission of the information owner and Senior Management. Confidential information must not be held on personal cloud storage services or removable storage (USB key, etc.). When information is held on mobile devices, it must be stored on Purpose approved cloud storage (e.g. OneDrive). All information stored on mobile or portable devices must be encrypted in line with Purpose Data Encryption Standards.

Equipment users must prevent intentional or inadvertent disclosures of sensitive information or access to information assets. This includes proactively locking computer screens and mobile devices before leaving them and preventing unobstructed views of computer screens.

## Communication Policies

### Official Corporate Communication

Only designated company spokespersons are authorized to speak on behalf of Purpose. At Purpose, such individuals are restricted to members of the Leadership team. All official corporate communications must be pre-authorized by the CEO or a designee before being released to coworkers or external parties.

### Media

Media contacts about the company and current and potential products, team members, partners, customers, and competitors should be referred to the CEO, a designee or a member of the Leadership Team. This does not specifically include opinions, writing, and interviews on topics aside from our company and our current and potential products, team members, partners, customers, and competitors.

### Internal Communication

It is strictly forbidden to post on Slack or by email a message that may discriminate against team members by virtue of any protected classification, including but not limited to, race, gender, nationality, sex, age and religion. Harassing messages of any sort will not be tolerated.

### External Communication

We understand that team members are passionate about what we do here at Purpose. We believe in open communication and encourage team members to tell the world about their daily lives at work. Whether choosing to do so by participating in a blog, social media networks, or any other form of online publishing or discussion, team members are representing the brand.

Team members should note that they remain responsible for what they publish online. In order to avoid issues or misunderstandings, team members are encouraged to exercise caution and common sense. Organizations need to ensure they convey the same message to every person, association, or organization they reach. This is key to maintaining consistency and credibility. If one of these points is breached, the company puts its reputation at risk and possibly its future viability. This policy will help keep Purpose's communications consistent with our values and ethics. It will ensure that the information contained in all communications to internal and external parties is consistent, timely and accurately reflects our position.
