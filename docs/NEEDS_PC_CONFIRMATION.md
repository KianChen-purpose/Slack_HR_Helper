## 3. Conflicting clawback terms — Learning/Training/Tuition

- **Section:** Learning & Growth / Training & Development / Tuition Support → Financial Terms & Commitment
- **Issue:** The playbook states the post-completion retention/clawback term two different ways:
  - *"Learning & Growth Investment"* applies the clawback to **any approved** learning investment, and says the **reimbursed amount** must be repaid on a prorated basis if the team member resigns or is terminated for cause within 12 months of course completion.
  - *"Training and Development"* applies the clawback only to **training exceeding $1,000**, and says the team member must pay the **total cost incurred** at a prorated amount.
  - Both use a 12-month retention window following course completion, but the **trigger threshold** ($0 vs. >$1,000) and the **repayment basis** (reimbursed amount vs. total cost incurred) differ.
- **Needed:** Decide and state a single authoritative clawback clause (threshold, repayment basis, retention period, and withdrawal/non-completion handling).
